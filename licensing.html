Licensing

Information on gambling licences, applications and regulatory requirements.

Licensing Services

The Gauteng Gambling Board is responsible for the regulation, licensing and monitoring of gambling activities within Gauteng. All gambling operators are required to obtain the relevant licence before commencing operations.

Licence Categories

🎰 Casino Licences

Licensing and regulation of casino operations.

🎲 Bingo Licences

Licensing of bingo operators and facilities.

🖥️ LPM Licences

Limited Payout Machine operator licences.

🏇 Betting Licences

Bookmaking and betting operator licences.

Application Process

Follow these steps when applying for a licence:

  • Complete the required application forms.
  • Submit all supporting documentation.
  • Pay the prescribed application fees.
  • Await regulatory assessment.
  • Receive approval and licence issuance.

Required Documents

Identification

Certified copies of identification documents.

Business Registration

Company registration documentation.

Financial Information

Supporting financial statements and records.
