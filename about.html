About the Gauteng Gambling Board

Regulating and monitoring gambling activities in Gauteng.

Who We Are

The Gauteng Gambling Board is a statutory body responsible for regulating, monitoring and overseeing gambling activities within the Gauteng Province. The Board aims to ensure that gambling remains fair, transparent and socially responsible.

🎯 Mission

To regulate and monitor the gambling industry in a fair, transparent and responsible manner.

👁️ Vision

To be a leading gambling regulator that promotes public confidence and responsible gambling.

⭐ Values

Integrity, Accountability, Transparency, Professionalism and Service Excellence.

Promote responsible gambling.

Public Protection

Protect the public interest.

Our History

The Gauteng Gambling Board was established to regulate and oversee gambling activities within Gauteng Province. Through effective regulation and monitoring, the Board seeks to promote public confidence, ensure compliance, and encourage responsible gambling practices.
