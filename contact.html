Common questions

Send us a message using the form above and choose "Complaint or dispute", or call us on (011) 581 4800. Please include your contact details and as much information as you can about what happened.

Call the toll-free helpline on 0800 006 008. It is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. You can also read more on our Responsible Gambling page.

Self-exclusion lets you ask to be barred from gambling venues. Contact us by phone or through the form, and choose "Responsible gambling" as the subject, and we will explain how it works.

Use the form above and choose "Licensing", or call (011) 581 4800. You can also visit our Licensing page.
